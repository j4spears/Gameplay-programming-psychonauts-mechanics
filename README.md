In this second year university project, we we're asked to create 4 mechanics (and subsequently a level) for the 3d platformer, Psychonauts.
I chose to theme my level around casinos, thus my main mechanic was a 3rd person shooter based on blackjack. Shooting (dealing) cards at 
enemies to increase their score. If their score exceeds 21 they die (bust). But be careful, if their score exceeds your score (while remaining under 21)
they will continually dash at the player. *WIP*
